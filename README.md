# The Parent Trap
Description
: **The Parent Trap** is a beloved family comedy about identical twin sisters, Hallie Parker and Annie James, who were separated shortly after birth when their parents, Nick and Elizabeth, divorced. Raised on separate continents—Hallie in California with her father and Annie in London with her mother—the girls accidentally meet at a summer camp in Maine. After realizing they are sisters, they decide to switch places to experience the life with the parent they never knew and, most importantly, to execute a mischievous scheme to reunite their estranged parents and stop their father from marrying his gold-digging fiancée.
